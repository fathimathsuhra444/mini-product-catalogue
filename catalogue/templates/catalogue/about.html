About Mini Catalogue

A simple and user-friendly product management website developed using Python and Django to help users explore and manage products in one place.

Welcome to Mini Catalogue

Mini Product Catalogue is a web-based application developed using Python and the Django framework. It provides an organized way to display and manage different products.

Products can include smartphones, laptops, headphones, and other electronic items. Each product can contain a name, category, price, description, stock information, and image.

Our goal is to provide a simple interface that makes browsing and managing product information convenient for users.
